Two different sampling programs

Described by program staff · November 10, 2026. Confirm current practice with the county.

Freshwater in the river

Staff sample around the storm peak and afterward until tests show clean water. They try to get out as early as possible. There is no hours-after-peak deadline for these river samples.

Water in the bay

The separate storm-response program calls for sampling within six hours after the river peak during a qualifying pollution-closure event. Enough tide to reach the stations and laboratory availability also matter.

The page checks for a saved snapshot every five minutes. Public delivery can take longer; a successful download does not make old readings new. Source observation ages and snapshot age are shown separately.
